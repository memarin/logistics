Relay product roadmap

Product goal

Help warehouse managers coordinate outbound orders and resolve stock exceptions with explained actions and clear human authority.

Start with one small warehouse team. Validate coordination effort before wider system access.

Top three agent functions

FunctionValue hypothesisHuman boundary
Evidence-led stock investigationReduce manual evidence collection and expose missing or conflicting source records.Staff confirm quantity and condition. The agent does not invent evidence.
Fulfillment recovery planningReduce time from a shortage to a feasible, recorded decision.Managers approve the exact correction or hold. Rejections require a new plan.
Adaptive execution and outcome checksExpose blockers, resume after human intervention, and check the actual result.IT restores availability. Staff do physical work. Managers approve shipment release.

Priority and dependencies

Validate the workflow before a real AI pilot. Validate proposal accuracy before a live operational connection.

Future pilots require agreed system access, data rules, real identity, model credentials, and an operational owner.

Shared JSON state and simulated roles suit this PoC. A production pilot needs a normalized schema and real authorization.

Current scope limits

No transport routes, billing, payments, customer portal, inbound workflow, real system connection, or real customer data.
